# Product Brief

## Working concept

A mobile-first Spotify interface that restores the tactile discovery and
album-oriented listening experience of browsing physical records.

## Problem

Streaming makes music convenient, but search, recommendations, playlists,
and individual-track controls weaken the experience of exploring a personal
collection and listening to albums as complete works.

## Core experience

The listener enters a record store style cabinet, manually fingers through
saved albums, pulls one out, and places it on a record player.

## Primary user

A Spotify Premium listener who values albums and wants to rediscover music
already saved in their collection.

## Initial platform

A mobile-first responsive web application that also works well on desktop.

## Success hypothesis

If saved albums are presented as a spatial, manually browsed collection,
listeners will rediscover forgotten albums and make more intentional
album-level listening choices.

## First prototype question

Can someone understand how to browse the cabinet, pull out an album, and
begin listening without instructions or search?
